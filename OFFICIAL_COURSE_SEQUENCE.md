# Official Course Sequence

This sequence is authoritative for the student website and future navigation updates.

1. Foundations → Theory Exam #1
2. Research, Intake, SOAP, Reflexology & Full-Body Routine → Theory Exam #2
3. Medical Massage, Pathology, Special Populations, Modifications & Pharmacology
4. Cumulative Review → Theory Exam #3 / Midterm
5. Manual Lymphatic Drainage → practical competency only
6. Deep Tissue & Clinical Techniques → Deep Tissue, Myofascial Release, Trigger Point Therapy, assessment, joint mobilization, RI/PIR and stretching
7. Ethics & Therapeutic Relationship → Theory Exam #5
8. Business & Professional Practice → Theory Exam #6
9. Additional Modalities
10. Final MBLEx Preparation

## Structural rules

- MLD follows the cumulative midterm and is not tested on a numbered theory exam.
- Myofascial release and trigger point therapy are separate techniques within the Deep Tissue & Clinical Techniques block.
- Trigger point therapy does not belong in the Ethics block.
- Ethics has its own dedicated exam.
- Business has its own dedicated exam.
- Medical Massage emphasizes conditions/pathologies, massage indications and contraindications, modifications, special populations, modalities used for condition-specific care, and pharmacology.
- Reviews must align directly with the content assessed on the exam that follows.
- Legacy filenames do not determine teaching order. The official sequence above does.
