Recognizing & Reporting Child Abuse

This page supports the required Pennsylvania training in your course. It isn't a replacement for the official mandated-reporter training or current Pennsylvania reporting instructions.

Your job isn't to investigate

A mandated reporter's role is to recognize reasonable concern, respond appropriately and follow the required reporting process. Don't interrogate a child, attempt to prove what happened, confront a suspected perpetrator, or delay a required report while trying to gather certainty.

When a child discloses

Stay calm. Listen without shock or blame. Don't promise secrecy. Use minimal, non-leading clarification only when needed. Avoid making the child repeat the story unnecessarily. Record relevant information according to policy and follow the current reporting procedure.

Massage-specific awareness

Because massage involves touch, privacy and partial undressing, therapists need especially clear consent, draping and professional boundaries with minors. Follow applicable rules for parent/guardian involvement while still respecting the minor client's dignity and communication.

Course requirement

Complete the Pennsylvania-approved online training assigned by the program and retain the completion certificate. Reporting laws and procedures can change, so use the current official training and state instructions rather than relying on an old handout.
