Palpation logic

Use the action to confirm the structure rather than digging for it. Ask for a gentle contraction, locate landmarks first, then soften the contraction before treatment. Protect axilla, anterior neck, cubital tunnel and other endangerment areas. Deep does not mean indiscriminate.
