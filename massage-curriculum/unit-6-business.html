Business Practices

A sustainable massage career requires clinical skill, professional communication, ethical judgment, financial planning, marketing, and systems that protect both therapist and client.

Employment models

Employee
Usually works under greater employer control and receives a W-2 with payroll withholding.
Independent contractor
Operates an independent business relationship and generally manages their own taxes. A contract label alone doesn't determine classification.
Sole proprietorship
Simple one-owner structure without a separate liability shield.
LLC
A state-law business entity that can provide liability separation. Tax treatment depends on ownership and elections.

Insurance

Professional liability addresses claims related to professional services and negligence. General liability addresses many premises-type risks such as a client slipping in the office. Product liability matters when products you sell or provide cause harm. Property, business interruption, cyber and disability coverage may also matter depending on the practice.

Money

Know startup costs, fixed and variable expenses, break-even point, pricing, revenue goals, taxes, recordkeeping and cash reserves. Self-employed therapists generally need to plan for estimated taxes rather than assuming an employer will withhold everything.

Policies create boundaries

Put fees, cancellation/no-show rules, late arrival policies, payment expectations, gift certificates, refunds, communication methods and professional conduct expectations in writing. Consistency prevents a surprising number of ethical and financial problems.

Marketing without nonsense

Describe what you actually do and the outcomes you can responsibly discuss. Don't diagnose, guarantee results, claim to cure disease, inflate credentials, or use client information without permission. Long-term retention comes from competent care, trust, communication, consistency and an experience clients want to return to.

Business project checkpoint

Build an executive summary, business concept, legal structure, location and zoning plan, target market, competitor analysis, service menu and pricing, startup budget, monthly expenses, break-even estimate, revenue projection, marketing plan, policies, insurance plan, tax plan and long-term goals.
