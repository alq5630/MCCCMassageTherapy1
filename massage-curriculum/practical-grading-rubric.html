Massage Practical Grading Rubric · 100 Points

A usable scoring model for full-routine checkoffs. Safety/boundary violations can override numerical score.

DomainPointsFull-credit standard
Preparation & intake10Prepared environment/supplies; reviews health changes, goals, contraindications and consent.
Draping & privacy15Secure, efficient, exposes only intended region; communicates before sensitive-region work; immediately honors consent changes.
Sequence & transitions15Performs required New Routine in logical order without major prompting; transitions smoothly and maintains contact appropriately.
Technique quality20Appropriate stroke mechanics, pressure, rhythm, tissue engagement and limb support; distinguishes broad vs specific work.
Anatomy & endangerment safety15Works on intended soft tissue, recognizes landmarks, avoids vulnerable structures and modifies appropriately.
Body mechanics10Stable stance, weight transfer, neutral/efficient joints, appropriate table height and tool selection.
Communication & responsiveness5Checks comfort/pressure without excessive interruption; responds appropriately to feedback.
Clinical judgment5Recognizes when to modify/stop/reassess; stays within scope.
Closing & professionalism5Intentional closing, safe transition off table, appropriate post-session communication/cleanup.

Suggested performance bands

90–100: independent, safe, coherent and professionally ready for course level. 80–89: competent with minor consistency issues. 70–79: developing; needs targeted remediation. <70: insufficient consistency or significant skill gaps.

Critical safety errors

Examples that may require automatic remediation regardless of point total: knowingly working a suspected DVT; unsafe neck/endangerment pressure; ignoring withdrawn consent; exposing sensitive areas improperly; dangerous table/equipment use; continuing after significant neurological symptoms; sexual/boundary misconduct; gross failure of infection-control practice.

Feedback format

Keep: one thing already strong. Change: one highest-priority correction. Practice: the exact drill/student action before reassessment. This keeps practical feedback actionable rather than overwhelming.
