CPR, AED & First Aid Readiness

Massage therapists work one-on-one with clients, often behind closed doors. Emergency readiness is part of professional safety.

This page isn't your certification course

Hands-on CPR/AED and first-aid skills must be learned and assessed through the program's approved certification training. Follow the current sequence, compression/ventilation instructions and emergency protocols taught by that certifying organization.

Before an emergency ever happens

Know your facility's address, emergency exits, AED location, first-aid kit location and how to summon help. Keep emergency contacts and relevant client information accessible while protecting confidentiality.

Massage-room emergencies

Take fainting, chest pain, difficulty breathing, stroke-like signs, severe allergic reaction, seizure, sudden altered consciousness and other potentially life-threatening symptoms seriously. Stop the session, assess according to your training, activate emergency services when indicated and provide care within your certification and scope.

Maintain certification

Track the expiration date instead of treating CPR as a one-time school requirement. Keep a copy of your current card with your professional records and renew before it expires when required for licensure or employment.
