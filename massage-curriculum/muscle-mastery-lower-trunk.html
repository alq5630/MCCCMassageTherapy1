Safety landmarks

Protect femoral triangle, medial proximal thigh, sciatic pathway, popliteal fossa, fibular head/common fibular nerve and superficial vessels. The IT band is connective tissue, not a muscle. Unexplained unilateral hot/swollen/tender calf is a vascular red flag, not a “tight calf.”
