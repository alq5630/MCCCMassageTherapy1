Reproductive, Pregnancy & Postpartum Conditions

Condition/contextCore conceptMassage reasoning
DysmenorrheaPainful menstruationMenstruation is not a contraindication; comfort/preferences and cause matter.
EndometriosisEndometrial-like tissue outside uterus associated with pain/inflammationMassage may support comfort but does not remove lesions or treat disease itself.
PCOSEndocrine/metabolic reproductive syndrome with ovulatory and androgen-related featuresNot itself a massage contraindication; avoid hormonal/fertility cure claims.
FibroidsBenign uterine smooth-muscle tumorsSymptoms/size/pregnancy/surgery status matter; do not attempt to “break up” fibroids.
PregnancyNormal physiological state with major body changesAdapt positioning, pressure and risk screening. Not automatically high-risk or contraindicated.
Preeclampsia concernPregnancy hypertensive disorder that can be seriousConcerning symptoms/high-risk presentation require medical management, not massage as treatment.
Pregnancy DVT concernPregnancy/postpartum increase venous thromboembolism riskUnilateral leg swelling/pain/warmth requires prompt medical evaluation.
PostpartumRecovery period after birth with vascular, hormonal and tissue changesConsider delivery type, bleeding, infection, DVT risk, fatigue and positioning.
Cesarean recoveryMajor abdominal surgeryRespect healing stage, incision/scar status and medical restrictions. Scar work only after appropriate healing/training.
MastitisBreast inflammation, sometimes infectious, often during lactationSystemic illness/infection requires appropriate healthcare; breast work requires specific scope, consent and training.
Pelvic inflammatory diseaseInfection/inflammation of upper female reproductive tractAcute infection requires medical treatment, not abdominal massage.

Professional boundary

Massage therapists can adapt comfort and musculoskeletal care around reproductive health. They do not diagnose pregnancy complications, perform internal reproductive examinations, or make fertility/hormonal treatment claims outside scope.
