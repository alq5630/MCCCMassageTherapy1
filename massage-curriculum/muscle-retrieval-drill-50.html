Muscle Retrieval Drill · 50 Prompts

Say the answer aloud before opening it. Retrieval beats rereading.

1. What nerve innervates deltoid?
Show answer

Axillary nerve.

2. Primary action of middle deltoid?
Show answer

Shoulder abduction.

3. Supraspinatus nerve?
Show answer

Suprascapular.

4. Infraspinatus primary action?
Show answer

Lateral rotation of humerus.

5. Subscapularis primary rotation?
Show answer

Medial rotation.

6. Teres minor nerve?
Show answer

Axillary.

7. Serratus anterior nerve?
Show answer

Long thoracic.

8. Rhomboids nerve?
Show answer

Dorsal scapular.

9. Latissimus dorsi nerve?
Show answer

Thoracodorsal.

10. Teres major inserts where?
Show answer

Medial lip of intertubercular sulcus of humerus.

11. Pectoralis minor inserts where?
Show answer

Coracoid process.

12. Subclavius acts on what bone?
Show answer

Clavicle.

13. Biceps inserts primarily where?
Show answer

Radial tuberosity, plus bicipital aponeurosis.

14. Brachialis inserts on what bone?
Show answer

Ulna.

15. Coracobrachialis nerve?
Show answer

Musculocutaneous.

16. Triceps insertion?
Show answer

Olecranon.

17. Pronator teres action?
Show answer

Forearm pronation; weak elbow flexion.

18. Pronator quadratus nerve?
Show answer

Anterior interosseous branch of median nerve.

19. Supinator action?
Show answer

Forearm supination.

20. Brachioradialis strongest elbow-flexion position?
Show answer

Forearm near neutral/thumb-up.

21. SCM unilateral rotation direction?
Show answer

Contralateral rotation.

22. Splenius unilateral rotation direction?
Show answer

Ipsilateral rotation.

23. Suboccipital nerve is posterior ramus of?
Show answer

C1.

24. Primary muscle of inspiration?
Show answer

Diaphragm.

25. Diaphragm nerve roots?
Show answer

Phrenic nerve, C3-C5.

26. Masseter action?
Show answer

Elevates mandible; assists protrusion.

27. Temporalis posterior fibers do what?
Show answer

Retract mandible.

28. Rectus abdominis action?
Show answer

Trunk flexion and abdominal compression.

29. External oblique rotates trunk which direction?
Show answer

Contralaterally.

30. Internal oblique rotates trunk which direction?
Show answer

Ipsilaterally.

31. QL attaches superiorly to what rib?
Show answer

12th rib.

32. Iliacus nerve?
Show answer

Femoral.

33. Psoas major distal insertion?
Show answer

Lesser trochanter via iliopsoas tendon.

34. Glute max nerve?
Show answer

Inferior gluteal.

35. Glute med/min nerve?
Show answer

Superior gluteal.

36. TFL inserts into what connective structure?
Show answer

Iliotibial tract.

37. Piriformis general action at extended hip?
Show answer

Lateral rotation.

38. Quadriceps nerve?
Show answer

Femoral.

39. Which quadriceps crosses hip and knee?
Show answer

Rectus femoris.

40. Sartorius origin?
Show answer

ASIS.

41. Most hamstrings nerve division?
Show answer

Tibial division of sciatic nerve.

42. Hamstring exception?
Show answer

Short head biceps femoris uses common fibular division.

43. Most medial-thigh adductors nerve?
Show answer

Obturator.

44. Adductor magnus has what dual innervation?
Show answer

Obturator to adductor part; tibial division sciatic to hamstring part.

45. Gastrocnemius crosses which two joints?
Show answer

Knee and ankle.

46. Soleus crosses which joint?
Show answer

Ankle only.

47. Tibialis anterior actions?
Show answer

Dorsiflexion and inversion.

48. Fibularis longus/brevis nerve?
Show answer

Superficial fibular.

49. Fibularis tertius nerve?
Show answer

Deep fibular.

50. Popliteus key action?
Show answer

Helps unlock the knee at initiation of flexion.
