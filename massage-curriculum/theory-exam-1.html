Theory Exam #1

This page is the exam-readiness checkpoint. The actual graded exam remains instructor managed rather than exposing an answerable course exam publicly.

Exam content map

Tools of the Trade
Tables, upholstery, linens, lubricants, room setup and equipment care.
Body Mechanics
Stances, neutral alignment, center of gravity and injury prevention.
Positioning & Draping
Prone, supine, side-lying, semireclining, bolstering and secure draping.
Massage Techniques
Pressure variables and E-P-F-C-T-V technique families.
Infection Control
Hand hygiene, transmission, sanitation and standard precautions.
Contraindications
Absolute versus local contraindications, modifications and endangerment sites.

Before you take it

You should be able to define the major terms without prompts, identify the correct technique from a description, distinguish safe from unsafe practice, explain why a modification is needed, and apply the concepts to a short client scenario.

Review Unit 1 again

Practical connection

Theory isn't separate from hands-on work. When you perform the back routine, identify your stance, technique family, pressure, direction, rhythm, anatomical target, contraindication considerations, and draping decisions as you work. If you can explain your choices while maintaining a smooth session, you're integrating the unit rather than memorizing it.
