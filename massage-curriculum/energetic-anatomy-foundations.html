Energetic Anatomy Foundations

Why this is here

The MBLEx content outline includes concepts of energetic anatomy. Entry-level preparation should recognize common terminology and conceptual frameworks without presenting them as equivalent to established anatomical structures.

Qi / Chi and meridians

Traditional Chinese medicine describes qi as vital energy and uses channels commonly called meridians. Acupressure and related systems use specific points along these conceptual pathways. These are traditional energetic concepts, not nerves, blood vessels or lymphatic vessels.

Yin and Yang

Yin and yang describe complementary, interdependent qualities used in traditional Chinese frameworks. They are relational concepts rather than anatomical tissues.

Five-element framework

Traditional Chinese medicine commonly uses five phases/elements: wood, fire, earth, metal and water. Students may encounter associations among these phases, organ systems and patterns in traditional theory.

Chakras

Yoga and Ayurvedic traditions describe energy centers commonly called chakras. A commonly taught seven-chakra model places centers along the midline from pelvic/base regions toward the crown. Chakras are part of an energetic/spiritual framework rather than structures visible in standard anatomical dissection.

Ayurvedic concepts

Ayurveda describes constitutional patterns called doshas, commonly vata, pitta and kapha. These concepts belong to the traditional Ayurvedic system and should not be confused with biomedical diagnoses.

Professional reasoning

A massage therapist can respectfully discuss a modality's traditional framework while accurately distinguishing tradition from biomedical evidence. Client beliefs deserve respect, but practitioners should not use energetic terminology to diagnose disease, discourage medical care or promise cures.

MBLEx strategy

For entry-level questions, recognize the vocabulary and the tradition it belongs to. If a question asks for a biomedical structure, do not substitute an energetic concept. If it asks about meridians, qi, chakras or doshas, identify the appropriate traditional framework.
