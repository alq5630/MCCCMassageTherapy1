Usui Reiki · Part Two

Partner practice, self-Reiki, session flow, professional integration and Level 1 attunement.

Self-Reiki practice

Move through comfortable hand positions at the head, neck, chest, abdomen and other areas as taught. Use the sequence as a meditative self-practice rather than treating the traditional organ or emotional associations as medical diagnoses.

Partner session

  1. Prepare a quiet, comfortable environment and explain what the receiver can expect.
  2. Confirm whether light touch, hands-off practice, or a combination is preferred.
  3. Center yourself with relaxed breathing.
  4. Work through the taught hand-position sequence without forcing an experience.
  5. Allow silence and avoid interpreting every sensation for the receiver.
  6. Close the session calmly, allow time to reorient, and invite neutral feedback.

Attunement

Level 1 attunement is taught as a teacher-led ritual within the Usui Reiki tradition. Students should follow the specific attunement method demonstrated by their Reiki instructor rather than mixing multiple lineage procedures. The attunement is a spiritual/energetic initiation within the tradition, not a medical procedure or evidence of a healthcare credential.

Professional boundaries

Reiki doesn't change massage scope of practice. Don't diagnose chakra imbalance as disease, guarantee healing, tell clients to stop medication, or present intuitive impressions as medical facts. Obtain consent for touch and respect spiritual differences. A client never needs to share your beliefs for you to treat them respectfully.

Skills check

Students should be able to explain Reiki in neutral language, prepare the environment, demonstrate a basic session, use appropriate touch consent, distinguish traditional belief from medical evidence, and describe when Reiki should complement rather than replace conventional care.
