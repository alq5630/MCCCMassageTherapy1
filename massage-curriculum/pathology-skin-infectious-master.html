ConditionCore conceptMassage reasoning
PsoriasisImmune-mediated inflammatory skin diseaseNot contagious. Avoid cracked, bleeding or very irritated plaques.
Eczema/atopic dermatitisInflammatory itchy skin disorderModify around inflamed/excoriated skin; products may irritate.
Contact dermatitisIrritant or allergic skin inflammationAvoid triggering product/contact; local avoidance if active.
Tinea corporis/pedisContagious dermatophyte fungal infectionAvoid affected region and prevent cross-contamination.
ImpetigoContagious superficial bacterial infectionPostpone/local avoid depending distribution; hygiene and medical treatment matter.
CellulitisBacterial infection of deeper skin/subcutaneous tissueDo not massage infected area. Systemic symptoms increase urgency.
Herpes simplex active lesionsContagious viral lesionsAvoid active lesions; hand hygiene/standard precautions.
Herpes zoster/shinglesReactivation of varicella-zoster virusActive lesions and pain require caution; transmission can cause chickenpox in susceptible people. Avoid affected region and follow infection-control guidance.
WartsHPV-related skin growthsAvoid direct friction over lesion; prevent contamination.
AcneInflammatory pilosebaceous disorderAvoid aggressive friction over inflamed/pustular lesions.
BurnThermal/chemical/electrical tissue injuryAcute burns are not massage targets. Healed scar work depends on healing, sensation and training.
Pressure injuryLocalized skin/tissue injury from pressure/shearDo not massage damaged tissue; repositioning/medical wound care are different interventions.
Suspicious changing lesionNew/changing pigmented or nonhealing lesionDo not diagnose. Avoid traumatizing and recommend appropriate medical evaluation.

Infection clues

Increasing warmth, redness, swelling, pain, drainage/pus, fever or spreading streaking can suggest infection/inflammation. Massage therapists recognize concerning presentation and refer rather than deciding the organism.
