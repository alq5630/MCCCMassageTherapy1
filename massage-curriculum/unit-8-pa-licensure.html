Pennsylvania Licensure Roadmap

A planning guide for moving from massage school graduate to licensed professional. Requirements can change, so students should verify the current application instructions with the Pennsylvania State Board of Massage Therapy before applying.

Roadmap

1. Complete the approved education requirements.
Make sure the school has the information needed to verify your education.
2. Complete the required licensing examination process.
Follow current MBLEx/FSMTB instructions and make sure your identifying information matches across applications.
3. Complete Pennsylvania's application requirements.
Follow the Board's current checklist for application, background-related requirements, training documentation and fees.
4. Complete required safety/reporting training.
Your program includes child-abuse recognition/reporting training and CPR/first-aid certification preparation. Keep documentation.
5. Don't practice as licensed until the license is actually issued.
Graduation, passing an exam and submitting an application aren't the same thing as holding an active license.

Keep a professional records folder

Save school completion documents, exam information, CPR/first-aid cards, mandated-reporter training certificates, application confirmations, continuing-education certificates, insurance documents and license renewal records.

Renewal mindset

Licensure isn't a one-time task. Track renewal deadlines, continuing-education requirements and CPR status early. Don't wait until the final week of a renewal cycle to discover a missing certificate.
