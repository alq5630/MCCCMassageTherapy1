Student Course Roadmap

This is the recommended path through the website. Use the search page anytime you need to look up a specific answer.

How to use this course

For each topic: learn the theory → identify/palpate the anatomy → connect pathology and contraindications → practice the relevant hands-on technique → answer application questions → document/reassess. Do not wait until the end of the program to begin MBLEx review.

1 · Foundations

  • Medical Terminology & Anatomical Language
  • Cells, Tissues & Homeostasis
  • A&P Core Map
  • Kinesiology Core
  • Joint Anatomy & Movement

2 · Body Systems

  • Skeletal + Muscular
  • Integumentary + Nervous
  • Endocrine + Cardiovascular
  • Lymphatic/Immune + Respiratory
  • Digestive + Urinary + Reproductive

3 · Muscle Mastery

  • Upper Body OIA
  • Lower Body & Trunk OIA
  • Expanded Muscle Groups
  • For every muscle: location → origin → insertion → action → palpation → shortened/lengthened position → routine connection.

4 · Safety & Clinical Reasoning

  • Endangerment Sites Atlas
  • Contraindications Decision Guide
  • Pathology Encyclopedia
  • Pharmacology

5 · Technique & Assessment

  • Basic Techniques
  • Swedish Strokes
  • Soft Tissue & Technique Science
  • Hydrotherapy
  • Assessment, Pain, ROM & Posture
  • Treatment Planning, Consent & Documentation
  • Trigger Point Therapy + Myofascial Release

6 · Practical Routine

Learn each region as anatomy + technique + safety, then integrate it into the full sequence. Key current modules include back, glutes/posterior legs, anterior legs/feet, arms, and chest/neck/shoulders. Practice transitions and body mechanics as seriously as individual strokes.
