Endocrine, Respiratory, Digestive & Urinary Pathology

Endocrine/metabolic

Diabetes mellitusChronic hyperglycemia from insulin deficiency/resistanceScreen neuropathy, wounds, circulation, glucose stability and medication timing.
HypoglycemiaAbnormally low blood glucoseSweating, shaking, hunger, confusion can progress to seizure/unconsciousness; treat as urgent per emergency plan.
HyperthyroidismExcess thyroid-hormone effectHeat intolerance, tachycardia/tremor and medical stability matter.
HypothyroidismLow thyroid-hormone effectFatigue/cold intolerance may affect comfort; stable treated disease is not automatically contraindicated.
Cushing syndromeExcess cortisol effectSkin fragility, bruising, osteoporosis, weakness and metabolic issues can affect pressure/safety.

Respiratory

AsthmaChronic inflammatory airway disorder with variable obstructionPosition comfortably; acute severe attack is emergency, not massage treatment.
COPDPersistent airflow limitation, commonly emphysema/chronic bronchitis phenotypesPacing, positioning and breathing tolerance matter.
PneumoniaInfection/inflammation of lung tissueAcute systemic illness usually means postpone and medical management.
Acute respiratory infectionViral/bacterial illness affecting respiratory tractTransmission/systemic symptoms may require postponement.

Digestive

GERDReflux of gastric contents causing symptoms/complicationsPositioning after meals may matter; massage does not repair LES dysfunction.
Peptic ulcer diseaseUlceration of stomach/duodenal mucosaSevere abdominal pain, bleeding signs or acute symptoms need medical care; avoid aggressive abdominal work.
IBSDisorder of gut-brain interaction with abdominal pain and bowel changesSymptoms fluctuate; use consent/comfort and avoid cure claims.
IBDCrohn disease/ulcerative colitis: chronic inflammatory diseaseAcute flare, pain, fever, surgery/ostomy and medications alter plan.
Gallbladder inflammation/stonesBiliary disease causing upper-right abdominal symptomsAcute severe symptoms require medical evaluation, not deep abdominal massage.

Urinary

UTIInfection of urinary tractSystemic symptoms/fever/flank pain may indicate more serious infection and need medical care.
Kidney stonesUrinary calculi causing often severe colicky flank painAcute severe pain needs medical evaluation; massage does not mechanically move stones through ureter.
Chronic kidney diseaseProgressive loss of kidney functionFluid balance, edema, fatigue, skin, medications and dialysis access may affect massage.
Dialysis accessAV fistula/graft or catheter used for dialysisProtect access site from compression/trauma; follow client medical guidance.
