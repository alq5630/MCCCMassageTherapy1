Usui Reiki · Part One

An introduction to Reiki as a Japanese-derived complementary energy practice, including its traditional history, principles, energetic framework, practitioner preparation and basic session structure.

Mikao Usui & the tradition

Mikao Usui is identified within the Reiki tradition as the founder of the Usui system in the early twentieth century. Stories about his spiritual experience and the origins of Reiki are part of Reiki tradition and should be presented as tradition rather than established historical or scientific fact.

The five Reiki principles

Just for today: don't be angry; don't worry; be grateful; do your work with integrity; and be kind to living things. The principles frame Reiki practice around mindfulness, gratitude, compassion and ethical intention.

Traditional energetic concepts

Ki
Japanese concept of life-force energy.
Chakras
Energy centers described in South Asian spiritual traditions and commonly incorporated into modern Reiki teaching.
Breath
Used to settle attention and support a calm practitioner state.
Tanden
A Japanese energetic center used in centering practices.

Seven-chakra review

Root: grounding and security. Sacral: emotion, pleasure and creativity. Solar plexus: confidence and personal power. Heart: love and connection. Throat: communication and truth. Brow: intuition and discernment. Crown: spiritual connection and unity.

Reiki session basics

The receiver remains clothed and may lie on a massage table or sit comfortably. The practitioner uses a sequence of hands-on or hands-above-body positions. Explain touch and obtain consent just as you would with any other service. Reiki is complementary care. Don't use energetic explanations to diagnose disease, promise cures, or discourage appropriate medical care.
