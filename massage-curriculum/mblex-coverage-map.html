MBLEx Content Coverage Map

Use this to verify that every official content domain has a teaching path. This course goes beyond the outline in many places, but nothing on the outline should be orphaned.

11% · Anatomy & Physiology

  • All listed body systems: dedicated system chapters
  • Tissue injury/repair: Cells, Tissues & Homeostasis
  • Sensory: Sensory Systems
  • Energetic anatomy: Energetic Anatomy Foundations

12% · Kinesiology

  • Muscle components/contractions/proprioceptors: Kinesiology Core
  • Locations, origins, insertions, actions: regional muscle mastery pages
  • Innervation added as MCCC syllabus depth: Innervation Master
  • Joint structure/function and ROM: Joint Anatomy + ROM Reasoning

14% · Pathology / Contraindications / Special Populations

  • Overview/pathologies: Pathology Encyclopedia + Decision Matrix
  • Site/pathology contraindications and caution: Decision Guide + Endangerment Atlas
  • Tools/special applications: Tools Safety
  • Special populations: Special Populations
  • Medication classes: Medication Safety

15% · Benefits & Effects

  • Physiological/psychological effects + techniques/sequence: Soft Tissue & Technique Science
  • Hot/cold: Hydrotherapy
  • Modalities: Modalities Comparison
  • Actual technique integration: New Routine regional labs

17% · Assessment / Reassessment / Treatment Planning

  • Intake: Health History
  • Written data/SOAP: SOAP Lab
  • Visual/posture/gait/palpation: Assessment Master
  • ROM: ROM Reasoning
  • Clinical reasoning/goals/previous response/strategy: Treatment Strategy

16% · Ethics / Boundaries / Law

  • Ethical behavior, principles, therapeutic relationship, dual relationships, sexual misconduct, confidentiality, scope and communication: Ethics, Boundaries & Law
  • Applied consent/boundaries: Draping & Boundaries Lab
  • Actual wording: Communication Lab

15% · Professional Practice

  • Equipment/supplies/facility safety: Equipment & Facility Safety
  • Hygiene/sanitation/PPE: Standard Precautions lesson
  • Body mechanics/self-care/injury prevention: Body Mechanics
  • Draping: Draping Lab
  • Business planning/office/marketing/hiring/records: Business Operations
  • Healthcare/business terminology: Terminology

Testing layer

Question Banks 1–4, 50-prompt muscle retrieval, 100-question mock exam and rapid review provide repeated retrieval across domains. Missed questions should send students back to the linked teaching layer rather than simply memorizing the answer.
