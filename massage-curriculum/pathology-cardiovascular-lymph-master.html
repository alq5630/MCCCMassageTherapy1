Cardiovascular, Blood & Lymph Pathology

ConditionCore conceptMassage decision
DVTThrombus in deep vein, commonly legSuspected DVT: no massage, prompt medical evaluation.
Pulmonary embolismEmbolus obstructing pulmonary circulationSudden dyspnea/chest pain/collapse can be emergency.
Varicose veinsDilated superficial veins associated with venous valve dysfunctionAvoid aggressive direct pressure over affected veins.
Chronic venous insufficiencyImpaired venous return, often with edema/skin changesCause/severity and skin integrity matter; do not assume all edema needs massage.
HypertensionPersistently elevated arterial pressureConsider control, symptoms and medication effects. Severe symptomatic presentation needs medical care.
Hypotension/orthostasisLow pressure or pressure drop on standingSlow transitions; fall prevention.
AtherosclerosisPlaque disease of arterial wallsSystemic cardiovascular status matters; massage cannot mechanically clear plaques.
Coronary artery diseaseAtherosclerotic disease affecting coronary circulationStable medically managed clients differ from active chest-pain emergencies.
Heart failureHeart cannot meet body's circulatory demands adequatelySeverity, edema, dyspnea and medical stability determine appropriateness; decompensation needs medical care.
Raynaud phenomenonEpisodic digital vasospasmCold can provoke symptoms; thermal applications require caution.
AnemiaReduced hemoglobin/oxygen-carrying capacityFatigue, dizziness and underlying cause matter.
Hemophilia/bleeding disordersImpaired coagulationBleeding risk may make deep/aggressive pressure unsafe; coordinate appropriately.
ThrombocytopeniaLow platelet countBruising/bleeding risk depends on severity; may require substantial modification/medical guidance.
LymphedemaProtein-rich interstitial swelling due to impaired lymph transportSpecialized management; ordinary deep massage is not MLD.
LymphangitisInflammation/infection of lymphatic vesselsAcute infection needs medical treatment, not massage over region.
LymphomaMalignancy of lymphoid tissueConsider treatment status, fatigue, blood counts, ports/nodes and oncology context.
HIVRetrovirus affecting immune systemNot transmitted by ordinary intact-skin massage; standard precautions apply to everyone.

Edema reasoning

Edema is a sign, not a diagnosis. Possible causes include venous disease, lymphatic impairment, heart/kidney/liver disease, inflammation, injury and medications. The safe question is why is this swollen?, not “how do I push the fluid out?”
