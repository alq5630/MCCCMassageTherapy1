Reproductive System

Female system

Ovaries produce oocytes and hormones; uterine tubes transport the oocyte/are typical fertilization sites; uterus supports implantation and pregnancy; cervix connects uterus to vagina.

Cycle

Ovarian and uterine cycles are coordinated by hypothalamic-pituitary-ovarian hormones. Menstruation is shedding of the functional endometrial layer when pregnancy does not occur.

Male system

Testes produce sperm and testosterone; epididymis supports sperm maturation/storage; ductus deferens transports sperm; accessory glands contribute seminal fluid.

Pregnancy

Pregnancy creates progressive cardiovascular, respiratory, musculoskeletal and positional changes. Massage adaptation is based on trimester, symptoms, risk factors, comfort and provider guidance when indicated, not the assumption that pregnancy itself is a disease.

Boundaries

Reproductive anatomy does not authorize massage of intimate structures. Consent, draping, scope and professional boundaries govern all treatment.

Study method

Be able to identify the major structures, state their functions, connect the system to relevant pathology and explain how the information changes massage safety or treatment planning.
