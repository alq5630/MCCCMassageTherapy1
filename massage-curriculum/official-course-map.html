Official Course Map

The website's curriculum architecture: six written theory checkpoints plus hands-on competencies and MBLEx integration.

Assessment rules

Teach → practice → review → assess. A written exam only tests content assigned to that theory block. Practical modalities can be assessed through hands-on competency instead of being forced into a written exam.

  • MLD: taught adjacent to Medical Massage, but not tested on a numbered Theory Exam.
  • Deep tissue: broad clinical-technique family.
  • MFR and Trigger Point Therapy: separate techniques within the clinical/deep-tissue block.
  • Ethics: its own standalone theory block and exam.
