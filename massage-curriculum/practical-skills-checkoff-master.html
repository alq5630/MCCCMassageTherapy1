Practical Skills Checkoff Master

Use this as a competency sheet. Sequence alone is not a passing practical.

Before touch

Room/table/supplies prepared and equipment inspected.
Relevant intake and health changes reviewed.
Treatment goal clarified.
Contraindications/areas of caution screened.
Plan, positioning and relevant sensitive-area work explained.
Consent obtained.

Professional setup

Client enters/exits table safely.
Bolstering supports comfort and breathing.
Draping protects privacy and exposes only intended region.
Lubricant is dispensed hygienically.
Therapist stance/table height supports safe mechanics.

Technique quality

Pressure is intentional and responsive rather than automatically deep.
Broad strokes establish/integrate before and after specific work.
Bony landmarks and endangerment sites are recognized.
Client limbs/head are supported during movement.
Therapist uses body weight and appropriate tools rather than thumb/wrist strain.
Transitions remain smooth and draping is restored before moving on.

New Routine sequence

Opening and scalp sequence complete.
Full-back warmup and side-to-side coverage complete.
Low-, mid-, upper-back and back closeout complete.
Glute/posterior leg sequence complete bilaterally as planned.
Turnover managed safely and professionally.
Anterior legs/feet complete.
Arms complete.
Chest/neck/shoulders complete with appropriate consent/draping.
Closing sequence complete without abrupt disengagement.

Clinical judgment

Student can name major muscles/landmarks under selected strokes.
Student can identify nearby endangerment sites.
Student modifies/skips when client response or contraindication requires it.
Student stops and reassesses neurological or other concerning symptoms.
Student does not diagnose or make unsupported physiological claims.

After treatment

Relevant goal/symptom/ROM reassessed when appropriate.
Client given safe time/privacy to transition off table.
Session documented objectively.
Linens handled and surfaces/equipment cleaned/disinfected appropriately.
Therapist performs hand hygiene.
